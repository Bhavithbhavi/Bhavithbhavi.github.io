Production · EdTech

GradeUp AI Platform

An AI learning platform that turns school textbooks into structured lessons, tutors students on them, and reviews their seminar slides while they work.

  • 14-agent LangGraph pipeline for textbooks: Mistral OCR, structure discovery, blueprint builder and judge, gap filler, schema validator, GPT-4o vision pass and a Google Slides agent.
  • Seminar co-pilot (LangGraph + Qdrant RAG + MCP) that watches a student's Google Slides edits, checks them against the textbook and applies fixes after human approval.
  • Hybrid RAG (Qdrant + Voyage AI + SearXNG) behind the AI Tutor, multi-user Debate, Seminar and English Teacher engines.
  • Adaptive learning: homework auto-assigned after 10 tutor questions on one topic, AI quiz grading and a unified student timeline, all traced in Langfuse.

Multi-agent · Generative video

AI Film Studio

Takes a short brief and produces a finished film: story, screenplay, cast, shots, voice, music and edit, with each step owned by an agent and checked by a quality gate.

  • Two LangGraph graphs split at a continuity gate: production (Story, Screenplay, Director, Casting, Production Design, Keyframes, Cinematography) and post-production (Voice, Editor, Music, Sound, Colour, Mix, Delivery).
  • Automated gates measure every image and clip (ArcFace face match, SAM 3, Grounding DINO, SigLIP 2, SSIM and frozen-frame checks) and hand repeated failures to a human reviewer.
  • 10 RunPod Serverless endpoints serving open models: Qwen3.6-27B on vLLM, Qwen-Image-Edit, LTX-2.5 video, Wan2.2 VACE, InfiniteTalk lip-sync, ACE-Step music, HunyuanVideo-Foley and FILM.

Speech · Localization

Dramaplex Dub Studio

Dubs drama videos into 11 Indic languages with speaker-matched voices, the original background music kept, and an optional human review of the translated script.

  • Vocal separation, WhisperX transcription, diarization, LLM + IndicTrans2 translation, TTS, then a time-aligned mix and remux in FFmpeg.
  • Timing enforced at three layers, from translation length budgets to trim-with-fade at mix time, so no dubbed line runs into the next.
  • Optional Wav2Lip lip-sync, automated QC report and resumable jobs on a remote GPU worker.
